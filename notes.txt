Toronto is the most populous city in Canada and the capital city \n of the Canadian province of Ontario.\
With a recorded population of 2,794,356 in 2021 \n it is the fourth-most populous city in North America.\
The city is the anchor \n of the Golden Horseshoe, an urban agglomeration of 9,765,188 people surrounding \
the western end of \n Lake Ontario. Toronto is an international centre of business, finance, arts, sports and\
culture, \n and is one of the most multicultural and cosmopolitan cities in the world.\

Moscow, on the Moskva River in western Russia, is the nation’s cosmopolitan \n capital. In its historic core is\
 the Kremlin, a complex that’s home to the president \n and tsarist treasures in the Armoury. Outside its walls \
is Red Square, Russia's \n symbolic center. It's home to Lenin’s Mausoleum, the State Historical Museum's comprehensive\
 collection \n and St. Basil’s Cathedral, known for its colorful, onion-shaped domes.

Rome is the capital city of Italy. It is also the capital of the Lazio region, \n the centre of the Metropolitan\
 City of Rome Capital, and a special comune named \n Comune di Roma Capitale

San Diego is a city on the Pacific coast of California known for its beaches, parks \n and warm climate. Immense \
Balboa Park is the site of the renowned San Diego Zoo,\n as well as numerous art galleries, artist studios, museums \
and gardens. A deep harbor \n is home to a large active naval fleet, with the USS Midway, an aircraft-carrier-turned-\
museum, \n open to the public.

Bangkok, Thailand’s capital, is a large city known for ornate shrines and vibrant street \n life. The boat-filled Chao \
Phraya River feeds its network of canals, flowing past \n the Rattanakosin royal district, home to opulent Grand Palace \
and its sacred Wat Phra Kaew \n Temple. Nearby is Wat Pho Temple with an enormous reclining Buddha and, on the opposite\
 shore, Wat \n Arun Temple with its steep steps and Khmer-style spire. 

Tokyo, Japan’s busy capital, mixes the ultramodern and the traditional, \n from neon-lit skyscrapers to historic\
 temples. The opulent Meiji Shinto \n Shrine is known for its towering gate and surrounding woods. The Imperial Palace \
sits amid large public \n gardens. The city's many museums offer exhibits ranging from classical art (in the Tokyo \
National Museum) to \n a reconstructed kabuki theater

Amsterdam is the Netherlands’ capital, known for its artistic heritage, \n elaborate canal system and narrow houses \
with gabled facades, legacies of the \n city’s 17th-century Golden Age. Its Museum District houses the Van Gogh Museum, \
works by Rembrandt and Vermeer \n  at the Rijksmuseum, and modern art at the Stedelijk. Cycling is key to the city’s\
 character, \n and there are numerous bike paths.\

Santorini is one of the Cyclades islands in the Aegean Sea. It was devastated  \n by a volcanic eruption in the 16th century\
 BC, forever shaping its rugged landscape. \n  The whitewashed, cubiform houses of its 2 principal towns, Fira and Oia, cling\
 to cliffs above an  \n underwater caldera (crater). They overlook the sea, small islands to the west and beaches made up of \
black, red and \n  white lava pebbles.

Seoul, the capital of South Korea, is a huge metropolis where modern skyscrapers, \n high-tech subways and pop culture \
meet Buddhist temples, palaces and street \n markets. Notable attractions include futuristic Dongdaemun Design Plaza,\
 a convention hall with \n curving architecture and a rooftop park; Gyeongbokgung Palace, which once had more than\
 7,000 rooms; \n and Jogyesa Temple, site of ancient locust and pine trees.

Colorado, a western U.S. state, has a diverse landscape of arid desert, river canyons \n and snow-covered Rocky Mountains,\
 which are partly protected by Rocky Mountain\n  National Park. Elsewhere, Mesa Verde National Park features Ancestral\
 Puebloan cliff dwellings. \n Perched a mile above sea level, Denver, Colorado’s capital and largest city, features a\ 
vibrant \n downtown area.
